The math

    

      For a single-sided position with spot at the top, the average fill price equals the
      geometric mean of the range bounds — so buying T tokens with
      U of quote (WETH or USDC) pins the bottom:
      
√(P_bottom · P_top) = U / T  ⟹  P_bottom = (U / T)² / P_top

      
Intuition knob: if you LP fraction f of the sale proceeds and want to rebuy 100% of what you sold, the bottom is simply P_bottom = f² · P_top — half the proceeds → quarter the price, 60% → 0.36×. (credit: Bob 🛸)

      
Denomination: the formula is unit-free — feed it all-ETH and the bottom is in ETH; all-USD and it's in USD. The pool trades RATSPEAK/WETH, so ETH is the native/auditable unit; USD mode just relabels and cross-displays via ETH/USD. Nothing about the on-chain position changes.

      
Liquidity L = U / (√P_top − √P_bottom). At spot the position is 100% quote; at the bottom it's
      100% token, having spent exactly U to accumulate exactly T. Feasible only when
      T > U / P_top (i.e. avg fill below spot). Execution rounds bounds to the pool's tick spacing —
      that's handled in the on-chain layer, decimals-aware.
