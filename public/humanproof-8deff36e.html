Written for you, formatted for your agent.

Every issue has the same shape: where it happened, what I did, what I expected, how bad it is, and a suggested fix. No essays to interpret.

  • Narrated screen recording, timestamped to each issue

Why not a testing panel?

Panels give you five strangers and a pile of video. You get one builder who has shipped products, and a list you can act on today.

Can't my agent test itself?

It can check that things run. It can't tell you a page made a person hesitate. That's the part I do.
